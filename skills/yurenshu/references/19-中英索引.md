# Bilingual Index · 中英索引

This cabinet makes the skill fully bilingual: an English query locates the rule via the table below, pulls cases from the Chinese cabinets, and returns an English prescription card. 本柜让整个系统中英双语可用：英文症状经下表定位条号与柜位，读中文案例库，输出英文开方卡。

**Translation policy**: the English layer speaks the language of management and medicine — diagnose, prescribe, remedy, dosage, contraindication. Classical Chinese names are kept as cultural flavor, but their English renderings use workplace vocabulary, never battle vocabulary. 策名保留中文的文化气质，英译一律用职场语言，不用战争语言。

## 2. The Four Principles · 心法四句（英文版）

1. **Fix the system before you schedule the talk.** A recurring problem is a missing rule, not a missed heart-to-heart.
2. **Give facts, not lectures.** One first-hand lesson beats ten persuasions; your job is to keep the cost of that lesson low.
3. **Praise in public, correct in private.** Run it in reverse and you win the room and lose the person.
4. **Reward the positive before punishing the negative.** Rewarding one right behavior shapes more than punishing ten wrong ones.

## 3. The Three Diagnostic Questions · 诊断三问（英文版）

1. **Can't, or won't?** Skill problems go to Cabinets 8–9 and 5 (tools, ladders, acceptance). Will problems go to Cabinets 6 and 7 (benchmarks, incentives, safety). Both-missing → Cabinet 4 (a controlled reality lesson), then the red lines.
2. **One person's problem, or the system's?** One case → single prescription. Three or more with the same symptom → structural: change the rule, not the individuals.
3. **Preventable upstream, or salvage downstream?** High-frequency problems get Cabinet 1–2 treatment first — kill the next occurrence before it happens.

## 4. Prescription Card (EN) · 开方卡英文模板

```
【Diagnosis】skill / will / structure — (one-line evidence)
【Primary】Cabinet N · Rule N: (remedy in English)
【Secondary】Rule N · Rule N
【Steps】1. … 2. … 3. … (specific to this week / this month)
【Script】"…" (natural English, not a literal translation of the Chinese script)
【Dosage】visible in N weeks; if no movement by N, escalate to …
【Contraindication】how this remedy backfires if overused
【Cases】[Enterprise] … [History] … [Cautionary] …
```

## 6. Sample English Scripts · 英文话术示例

**Scenario: veteran slacker (Rule 3)**
"Your experience is wasted sitting still in this seat. Next quarter you're moving to the new project — fresh field, fresh scoreboard, clean slate."

**Scenario: hiding errors (Rule 47)**
"You flagged it yourself, so this one's on the system, not on you — and that's noted. Covered-up mistakes get pursued; reported ones get paid for. That's the deal."

## 7. Citation Norms · 引用规范（英文输出时）

- **Companies**: official English names — Huawei, Haidilao, Pang Donglai, Chayan Yuese
- **Classics**: *Records of the Grand Historian* (Shiji), *Strategies of the Warring States* (Zhanguoce), *Zizhi Tongjian* (Comprehensive Mirror), *Thick Black Theory* (Houhei Xue)
- **People**: pinyin — Liu Bang, Zhang Liang, Li Mu; emperors by era name where customary (Emperor Wu of Han)
- **Remedy names**: workplace English + pinyin on first use — "Earn Trust First (limu quxin)" 立木取信 — then English only. Never render a remedy name in battle vocabulary; the classical Chinese flavor stays in the Chinese name, the English carries the management meaning
- **Four Classics novels**: *Romance of the Three Kingdoms*, *Journey to the West*, *Water Margin*, plus *Ming Chao Na Xie Shi'er* (Those Things in the Ming Dynasty, a bestselling popular history)
- **Drucker**: cite the book title in English — *The Practice of Management*, *The Effective Executive*
